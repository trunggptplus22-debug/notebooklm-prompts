# Research Synthesis to Video

Convert the supplied research synthesis into a visual narrative without changing its evidence status.

Identify the central question, key findings, competing explanations, and decision implications. Organize the video as: question → evidence map → mechanism → comparison → conclusion → unresolved test. Show source attribution for material claims where the format permits. Preserve uncertainty and doctrinal distinctions. Do not introduce facts, examples, numerical values, or causal links absent from the synthesis. Use visuals to reveal relationships and dependencies, not merely decorate each section.
