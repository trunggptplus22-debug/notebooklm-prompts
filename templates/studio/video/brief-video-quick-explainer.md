# Short Research Explainer Video

Create a concise technical explainer for [INSERT PROBLEM OR MECHANISM].

The video must communicate one central idea: the problem, the mechanism behind it, why existing approaches are insufficient, and the most useful insight or proposed solution. Use a clear sequence: problem framing → structural model → mechanism → concrete example → limitation → next test. Visuals should show actual relationships, state transitions, data flow, or equations—not decorative technology imagery. Keep terminology accurate and avoid oversimplifying contested rules. Mark hypotheses as hypotheses. Target 1–2 minutes.
