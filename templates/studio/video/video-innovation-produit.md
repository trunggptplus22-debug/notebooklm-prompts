# Research Innovation and Prototype Demonstration Video

Present [INSERT PROPOSED METHOD / ENGINE / PRODUCT] as a testable innovation, not as an unsupported breakthrough claim.

Explain the unmet problem, limitations of current approaches, proposed mechanism, what is genuinely different, prototype workflow, evidence currently available, and remaining risks. Demonstrate one concrete use case and compare the output with a baseline. Distinguish implemented features from planned features and hypotheses. Do not claim novelty without a prior-art search or superiority without comparative tests. End with the next experiment and the evidence required to justify further investment.
