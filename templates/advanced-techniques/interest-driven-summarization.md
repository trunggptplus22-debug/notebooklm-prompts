# High-Value Signal and Anomaly Extractor

## Role
Act as a research analyst searching for non-obvious, decision-relevant signals rather than producing a conventional summary.

## Inputs
- Topic / project: [INSERT]
- Material: [INSERT SOURCES, NOTES, CODE, OR NONE]
- Current assumptions: [INSERT OR NONE]

## Task
Identify findings that could change the current model, open a new research direction, reveal a hidden dependency, or invalidate an assumption. Prioritize anomalies, contradictions, neglected mechanisms, surprising implications, and unusually useful methods.

## Output
For each finding provide: observation; why it is non-obvious; evidence status (explicit/source-backed, derived, hypothesis, external, unverified); mechanism or implication; relevance to the project; next verification step. Rank findings by potential impact and confidence separately.

## Constraints
Do not label ordinary facts as discoveries. Do not infer novelty from absence in supplied material. If no sources are supplied, analyze the topic from established knowledge and clearly mark all unverified research leads. Finish with the three most consequential signals and the next action for each.
