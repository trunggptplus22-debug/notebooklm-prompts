# Claim-to-Reality Audit

## Objective
Evaluate whether a proposed method, tool, theory, or AI capability actually delivers the claimed result under realistic constraints.

## Inputs
- Claim or advertised capability: [INSERT]
- Intended use case: [INSERT]
- Available demonstration, code, data, or sources: [INSERT OR NONE]

## Audit
Translate each promise into an observable acceptance criterion. Identify the mechanism required for the promise to be true. Inspect evidence quality, test conditions, baseline comparisons, sample size, reproducibility, hidden human work, failure rates, dependencies, and scope limitations. Separate demonstrated capability from plausible inference, marketing language, and speculation.

## Output
For each claim: literal claim; operational meaning; required mechanism; available evidence; missing evidence; reproducibility; real-world constraints; verdict (verified / partially verified / unsupported / contradicted). Provide a minimal independent test and a decision: adopt, prototype, monitor, or reject.

Do not assume that a successful demo establishes general capability.
