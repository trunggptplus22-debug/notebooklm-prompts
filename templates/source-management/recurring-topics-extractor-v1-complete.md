# Cross-Source Consensus, Conflict, and Method Extractor

## Objective
Extract recurring mechanisms, shared principles, disagreements, and useful methods across a collection of sources.

## Inputs
- Sources: [INSERT]
- Research question: [INSERT]
- Scope / schools / versions to preserve: [INSERT]

## Procedure
For each source, identify its claims, definitions, assumptions, methods, evidence, and limitations. Normalize terminology only when equivalence is justified. Cluster recurring concepts and trace each cluster to its source locations. Identify contradictions, omissions, incompatible premises, and different levels of abstraction. Distinguish genuine consensus from repetition caused by shared citation or common origin.

## Output
- Source inventory and relevance.
- Concept / method clusters with citations.
- Consensus matrix.
- Conflict matrix with likely causes.
- Unique contributions and neglected mechanisms.
- Candidate synthesis and unresolved incompatibilities.
- Research questions generated by the comparison.

Do not infer consensus from frequency alone. Do not silently reconcile contradictions. Mark claims that require external verification.
