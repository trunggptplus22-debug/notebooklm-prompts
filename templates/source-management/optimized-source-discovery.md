# Targeted Research Discovery Query Builder

## Objective
Generate precise search strategies to discover methods, evidence, implementations, and unresolved research around a problem.

## Inputs
- Research question: [INSERT]
- Domain and adjacent fields: [INSERT]
- What is already known: [INSERT OR NONE]
- Desired evidence: papers / code / benchmarks / standards / historical sources / case studies

## Procedure
Decompose the question into mechanism, mathematical representation, algorithm, implementation, evaluation, and failure-mode searches. Generate queries across the exact domain and adjacent disciplines. Include synonyms, historical terminology, competing schools, and implementation vocabulary. Prioritize primary research, reproducible code, technical documentation, benchmark datasets, and critical replications.

## Output
For each query: exact search string; purpose; expected source type; likely discipline or venue; what evidence would answer the question; relevance criteria. Include queries designed to find contradictory evidence and failed approaches.

Do not claim a source exists or that a gap is established until it has been checked. Finish with a staged search plan: broad mapping, targeted mechanism search, implementation search, falsification search.
