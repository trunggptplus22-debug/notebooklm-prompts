# Research Capability Roadmap

## Objective
Design a capability-building roadmap around a concrete research or engineering outcome, not a generic course sequence.

## Inputs
- Target capability / deliverable: [INSERT]
- Current baseline: [INSERT OR UNKNOWN]
- Time and resource constraints: [INSERT]
- Preferred domain: [INSERT]

## Method
Decompose the target into prerequisite concepts, formal methods, domain knowledge, implementation skills, and verification skills. Identify the minimum knowledge needed to produce a working artifact. Separate essential prerequisites from optional depth. Use available sources when supplied; otherwise propose a targeted discovery plan and mark recommendations that require verification.

## Output
A staged roadmap with: learning objective; why it is needed; concepts and methods; practical exercise; artifact to produce; mastery test; dependencies; estimated effort; exit criterion. Include a capstone project that integrates the stages and exposes a real failure mode.

Prioritize learning by leverage and dependency order. Avoid recommending broad courses or reading lists without explaining the capability each item unlocks.
